### 🧬 About the Organization

**Suleiman MEGA Molecular Evolutionary Tree** is a specialized bioinformatics and computational evolutionary genetics initiative focused on high-resolution phylogenetic reconstruction, molecular clock calibration, and evolutionary distance modeling across vertebrate lineages.

Primary investigations leverage the **COL1A1** (*Collagen Type I Alpha 1*) marker conserved across >450 million years of vertebrate divergence, along with **TRO_Seq** epidemiologic datasets and animal comparative benchmarks.

### 🔬 Core Scientific Pipelines & Methodologies

* **Multiple Sequence Alignment (MSA):** Progressive multi-sequence alignment via embedded MUSCLE v3.8 in MEGA 12.
* **Model Selection:** BIC (Bayesian Information Criterion) substitution evaluation identifying **GTR+G+I** (&alpha; = 0.412, *I* = 0.318, &kappa; = 2.84).
* **Maximum Likelihood (ML) & Neighbor-Joining (NJ):** Robust evolutionary trees validated with 1,000 bootstrap iterations.
* **Molecular Clock Dating (RelTime):** Relative rate dating without strict clock assumptions, calibrated with Laurasiatheria divergence constraints (~87.5 Mya).
* **Interactive Tree Annotation (ITOL):** Publication-ready circular and rectangular cladograms with taxon color-coding and branch support values.
